The Activity Bar stays with the primary side bar and occupies its outer edge.
- [Official Custom Layout documentation](https://code.visualstudio.com/docs/configure/custom-layout) describes the same left/right relationship and the Activity Bar placement. The pinned source commit is the version authority; documentation supplements user-visible behavior.

## Scope

- Add a persisted primary side bar position, `left` or `right`, defaulting to `left` for layouts without this metadata.
- Register `workbench.action.toggleSidebarPosition` in the Command Palette and View > Appearance. Its menu label reflects the destination side.
- Move the existing primary side bar and Activity Bar as one layout cluster; in Side mode the Activity Bar remains on the outside edge. Keep Top, Bottom, and Hidden Activity Bar modes intact inside the cluster.
- Keep the secondary side bar opposite the primary side bar: right when primary is left, left when primary is right.
- Preserve the sidebar widths, Activity Bar state/size, collapsed/visible states, editor group ordering and weights, panel state, and other split-tree structure through toggles and JSON save/reload. Legacy JSON without the new position field defaults to left.

## Explicit exclusions

- Moving or assigning views between side bars, view-container menus, panel movement, drag/drop, and Reset View Locations.
- Splitting/collapsing views inside a side bar, the secondary side bar's default-visibility policy, and new settings.
- Changing panel position/alignment or adding an Activity Bar keyboard shortcut.
