## Source review and pre-edit graph analysis

`app/lib/workspace/workspace_watcher.dart` subscribes to `Directory(root).watch(recursive: true)`. `WorkspaceWatcher.stop()` invalidates callbacks, cancels its timer, and awaits `StreamSubscription.cancel()`.

In `app/lib/workbench/workbench_page.dart`, `_stopWorkspaceWatcher()` clears `_watcher` and queues `watcher?.stop()` after `_watcherTransition`. `dispose()` invokes `_stopWorkspaceWatcher()` without awaiting because Flutter `State.dispose()` is synchronous. The test helper unmounts the app, pumps 100 ms, then retries directory deletion up to five seconds. A still-open native directory watch is a plausible cause because the removed fixture contains the watched workspace, but the logs do not prove it is the owner. Other outstanding filesystem/process activity remains possible.
