# P05.79 / feat-131 / LAY-18b — Notification progress lifecycle

## Scope

Align progress notifications with the pinned Code-OSS progress service lifecycle:
- `NotificationProgress.done()` is terminal and notifies listeners once.
- A completed progress object no longer renders a progress line or cancel control; its notification remains in the notification center until dismissed/cleared.
- Cancellation is single-flight. While the callback is pending, a second cancel is ignored and the UI disables the button with a pending label.
- A failed cancellation clears pending and can be retried. Completion while cancellation is pending is terminal; the in-flight callback may finish but cannot re-enable cancel.
- Reports after completion are ignored.

No notification persistence or unbounded timer/worker was added. Progress remains event-driven through `ChangeNotifier`.
